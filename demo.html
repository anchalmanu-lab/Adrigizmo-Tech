From Pretoria Prodigy to Multi-Planetary Architect

Born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa, to Maye Musk and Errol Musk. At age 12, he taught himself computer programming and sold his first video game, Blastar.

He attended Pretoria Boys High School, moved to Canada in 1989 attending Queen's University, and later transferred to the University of Pennsylvania, graduating with dual degrees in Physics (BA) and Economics (Wharton BS).

After founding and scaling software ventures like Zip2 and X.com (PayPal), Musk redirected capital to fundamental civilizational challenges: energy transition, space exploration, and human-AI symbiosis.
